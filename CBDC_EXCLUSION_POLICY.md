# CBDC Exclusion Policy

The QMOOSA project portfolio must not generate, implement, deploy, advertise, or integrate central bank digital currencies (CBDCs), digital-rupee government issuance rails, or CBDC bridge adapters.

- All new projects must use decentralized non-CBDC assets and voluntary interoperability only.
- Reject manifests and generators that add CBDC-related project names, categories, APIs, SDKs, contract logic, or tokenomics.
- Preserve unrelated blockchain, wallet, cryptographic, stablecoin (non-CBDC), and decentralized finance capabilities.
- Treat historical provenance documents as records, not authorization to regenerate excluded projects.
- Existing CBDC-specific repositories and code require separate retirement/remediation; this policy does not imply they were deleted.
